BarLingo

A minimalist native translator that lives in the menu bar. Press one shortcut, type or dictate, and get the translation without leaving the app you are working in.

Translate without the detour

One shortcut. Two panes. Done.

BarLingo stays in the menu bar with no Dock icon by default. The configurable global shortcut opens a floating two-pane translator; swap languages, paste or type, then copy the result and return to your work.

Speak and listen

Dictate it. Hear it back.

Use native macOS dictation for the source text and read either side aloud with independently selected voices. Keyboard shortcuts keep dictation, clearing, reading and copying within reach.

Make it yours

Fits your language and your desktop.

Choose from 41 translation languages with right-to-left support and use the interface in 24 languages. Adjust position, size, transparency, glass intensity, colour scheme and opening animation — all without restarting.

More than translation

Small details that keep you moving.

Ready immediately

No registration or API key. Google’s public translation endpoint is used first, with MyMemory as an automatic fallback.

Your keyboard, your way

Configure the show-and-hide shortcut and optionally switch to a chosen keyboard input source when the window opens.

Accessible throughout

VoiceOver, keyboard navigation and localized spoken examples are supported across all 24 interface languages.

Private by design

No account. No profiling.

BarLingo has no analytics or advertising SDKs and no remote backend of its own. Only the text being translated is sent to the translation provider over HTTPS.

  • Local preferences remain on your Mac.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are used only for dictation.
  • Clipboard access is limited to paste and copy actions.
  • Global shortcuts do not require Accessibility permission.
