Wiksy

Wikipedia without the detour. Hit a global shortcut anywhere in macOS, type a name or a topic, and the article opens in its own floating window — readable, with quick actions to copy a section or open it in your browser.

  • ◆  Global hotkey from any app
  • ◆  Each article opens in its own window
  • ◆  Copy a section or the full article in one click
  • ◆  Native, lightweight, launches instantly

BarLingo

A native translator that lives in the menu bar and stays out of the way. Press a configurable global shortcut, type or dictate, and translate between 41 languages without an account, API key or setup.

  • ◆  41 translation languages, including right-to-left support
  • ◆  Native dictation and read-aloud on both sides
  • ◆  24 interface languages, switchable without restarting
  • ◆  Custom window, glass effect, colours and animations
